About Heena

Your Partner in Health & Wellness

Hello, I'm Heena

Certified Nutrition Coach & Women Wellness Specialist

My journey into wellness began with a personal struggle. After years of yo-yo dieting, failed weight loss attempts, and feeling disconnected from my own body, I decided to pursue proper education in nutrition and health coaching. That decision changed everything—not just for me, but for the hundreds of women I've had the privilege to coach.

Today, I combine evidence-based nutrition science with compassionate coaching to help women like you transform their health, confidence, and lifestyle.

My Story

The Struggle

For years, I battled with my weight, tried countless diets, and felt trapped in a cycle of restriction and failure. I didn't understand my body, my cravings, or my relationship with food.

The Change

I started learning about nutrition science, hormonal balance, and the psychology of eating. Everything changed when I stopped dieting and started living healthily.

The Certification

Motivated by my own transformation, I became a certified nutrition coach and women's wellness specialist to help others experience the same freedom.

The Mission

Today, I work with women to build sustainable habits, improve their health, and most importantly—regain confidence in their bodies and themselves.

My Mission & Philosophy

My Mission
