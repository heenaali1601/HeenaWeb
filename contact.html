Questions Before You Contact?

Is the consultation really free?

Yes! Your 30-minute consultation is completely free. No credit card needed, no hidden fees. It's my way of getting to know you and seeing if we're a good fit.

What happens during the consultation?

We'll discuss your health goals, current challenges, what you've tried before, and what you're looking for. Based on our conversation, I'll recommend the best program and answer all your questions.

What if I'm not ready to commit yet?

That's okay! The consultation is a chance to ask questions and explore options with no pressure. I want you to feel 100% confident before starting.

Can I book a consultation if I'm outside India?

Yes! I work with clients worldwide. We can do consultations via video call at a time that works for you.

What if I have medical conditions?

I work with people with various health conditions. During consultation, we'll discuss your medical history, and I can coordinate with your doctor if needed for certain conditions.

How do I pay for programs?

Payment details are discussed during consultation. I offer flexible payment options to make programs accessible to everyone.
