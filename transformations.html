Questions About Results?

How fast will I see results?

Most clients notice changes within 2-3 weeks (energy, sleep, mood) and significant weight loss within 4-6 weeks. However, sustainable results take time.

Will the weight come back?

92% of our clients maintain their results because they learn sustainable habits, not just follow a diet. The transformation is lifestyle-based, not temporary.

Are these results typical?

Yes! While individual results vary, most clients who follow the coaching achieve similar or better results. It depends on consistency and following the personalized plan.

What if I don't have 3-6 months?

You'll see results faster if you commit fully, but lasting change takes time. We focus on sustainable transformation, not quick fixes.
